# NovaMart Retail Business Performance Analytics — Business Insights

This folder contains evidence-based business insights derived from the validated analytical outputs and Excel dashboard.

## Objective

Translate analytical findings into clear, actionable business insights for management decision-making.

## Insight Areas

- Sales performance
- Profitability
- Product performance
- Category and subcategory performance
- Regional performance
- Customer and segment performance
- Discount effectiveness
- Business trends

## Insight Framework

Each insight should include:

1. Business observation
2. Supporting metric
3. Business implication
4. Recommended action

## Evidence Standard

- Insights must be supported by calculated data.
- No fabricated results.
- No unsupported causal claims.
- Metrics must reconcile with the analytical outputs.
- Recommendations must be practical and business-focused.

## Workflow

Validated Data → Analysis → KPI Evidence → Business Insight → Recommendation → Management Action
