# NovaMart Retail Business Performance Analytics — Insights

This folder contains evidence-based business insights derived from the validated analytical outputs and Excel dashboard.

## Objective

Translate analytical findings into clear, actionable business insights for management decision-making.

## Insight Areas

- Sales performance
- Profitability
- Product performance
- Category and subcategory performance
- Regional performance
- Customer performance
- Discount effectiveness
- Business growth opportunities
- Business risks

## Insight Framework

Each insight should include:

1. Business observation
2. Supporting metric
3. Business implication
4. Recommended action
5. Expected business impact

## Evidence Standard

All insights must be supported by validated analytical outputs.

No unsupported assumptions or fabricated findings should be presented as actual business results.
